# Demo video, 3 minutes 20

Xano asks for 2 to 4 minutes end to end.

**Before recording.** Run both demo questions once so they answer from the
ledger. Both currently cost nothing and return in about 16 seconds, which is
what makes this filmable in one take.

- "Under $2,800, one bedroom, no more than 25 minutes by bike to 2788 San Tomas Expressway, gym within half a mile open before 6am, in-unit laundry, grocery open past 10pm."
- "2 bed apartment, 10 min bike to San Jose State University"

Check the SerpApi balance after the warm up. If both say 0 spent, you are ready.

---

**0:00 to 0:20. The problem, over the landing page.**

> "When you move somewhere you don't know, every listing looks equally
> plausible. The rent is on the listing. The commute is on a map. The gym's
> opening hours are somewhere else again. So you end up with fifteen tabs open,
> checking each place by hand."

**0:20 to 0:45. Ask.**

Type the question. Say it out loud as you type.

> "You ask it the way you'd ask someone who lives there."

**0:45 to 1:15. The plan, before it runs.**

Point at the left rail while stages tick over.

> "Before it spends anything it works out the order to check things in. Free
> filters first, then one call per neighbourhood, then per listing only for
> what survived. Checking all of this the naive way is thirteen thousand
> calls. This plan is seventy six."

**1:15 to 2:00. The answer, and the third bucket.**

Show verified. Open one card.

> "Every fact says where it came from and how old it is. That's the real ride,
> door to door, measured on the road."

Then click Unconfirmed. This is the most important twenty seconds in the video.

> "This is the part I care about. It didn't guess and it didn't quietly drop
> them. A price quoted as a range settles nothing against a cap, so it says so.
> Nothing is ever ruled out because a check failed."

**2:00 to 2:20. The map.**

Open a card and show the route drawn to the destination.

> "That's the actual road, not a straight line."

**2:20 to 2:45. Show your working.**

Open the working panel.

> "Every source it used, what it cost, and whether those numbers are measured
> from real runs or still an estimate. It tells you which."

**2:45 to 3:05. Say why this is not a chatbot.**

Worth twenty seconds, because every judge is thinking it.

> "A model reads your sentence and nothing else. It never sees a listing and it
> never decides what passes. The checking is ordinary code against real sources,
> which is why it can tell you what it could not confirm instead of guessing."

**3:05 to 3:20. The ledger, and close.**

Ask the same question again. It returns fast and spends nothing.

> "Facts are stored per fact with their own expiry, so asking again costs
> nothing. Built on Xano, with SerpApi behind every answer."

---

**Two things to avoid.** Don't ask a brand new question on camera: it will spend
credits and take minutes waiting on the providers. Don't skip the unconfirmed
bucket to save time, it is the thing no competitor shows.
